# Expense Policy

## Travel Expenses

Employees may book economy-class flights for domestic travel. Business class is permitted for
international flights exceeding 6 hours. Hotel accommodation should not exceed $200 per night
in standard markets and $350 per night in high-cost cities (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo).

## Meals and Entertainment

Daily meal allowance is $75 for domestic travel and $100 for international travel. Client
entertainment requires pre-approval for expenses exceeding $500. All meals and entertainment
expenses must include itemized receipts.

## Software and Subscriptions

Individual software purchases under $100 per month do not require approval. Team-wide
subscriptions must be approved by the department head. All software must be vetted by IT
security before installation.

## Reimbursement Process

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Reimbursements are processed
bi-weekly on the 1st and 15th of each month. Late submissions (over 60 days) require VP approval
and may be denied.
