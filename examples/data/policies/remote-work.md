# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their probationary period (90 days) are eligible
for remote work. Contractors and part-time employees may request remote work on a case-by-case
basis with manager approval.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and monitor for remote workers. Employees are responsible for
maintaining a reliable internet connection with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps download. IT support
is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST.

## Communication

Remote employees must be available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM in their local timezone).
All team meetings should be attended via video call. Slack is the primary communication channel
for day-to-day work. Response time for messages should be within 2 hours during work hours.

## Security

All work must be performed on company-issued devices. VPN must be active when accessing internal
systems. Employees must not use public Wi-Fi without VPN protection. Sensitive documents must
not be printed at home without prior approval.
